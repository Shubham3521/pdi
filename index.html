💼 Things to Bring

💡 Important Tips

  • Go early morning — inspect the car in daylight to spot paint defects easily.
  • Do PDI before signing any papers — once you sign, complaints are harder to raise.
  • Check the VIN on chassis, dashboard, and documents — all must match.
  • Manufacturing date should be within 3 months of delivery. Reject if older than 6 months.
  • Odometer should read under 20-30 km — anything over 50 km is a red flag.
  • Take photos & videos of everything — timestamp proof protects you later.
  • Don't rush — a thorough PDI takes 60–90 minutes. The dealer must allow it.
  • Check the car OUTSIDE the showroom — parking lot lighting hides defects.
  • Note every issue in writing and get dealer acknowledgment before delivery.
